# 🥊 THAPRANO KORMOSHUCHI (থাপড়ানো কর্মসূচি)

An interactive, responsive 2D arcade slapstick web game built with HTML5 Canvas, modern CSS, and procedurally synthesized Web Audio.

## 🌟 Game Overview

Play as the **Muscular Bodybuilder** and test your power and timing against the **Bearded Guy in White Punjabi**!

### 🎯 3 Interactive Game Modes (গেম মোড)
1. **🏆 Campaign Mode**:
   - 4 story-driven missions with unique objectives, countdown timers, and tactical boss defense AI:
     - **Level 1**: *মিটিং রুমে বিচার* (Room Showdown) — 500 HP, 45s timer.
     - **Level 2**: *টং দোকানের মুখোমুখি* (Tong Confrontation) — 800 HP, 40s timer.
     - **Level 3**: *ছাদে ঘেরাও* (Rooftop Standoff) — 1,200 HP, 35s timer.
     - **Level 4**: *লঞ্চঘাটে চূড়ান্ত বিচার* (Final Reckoning) — 1,800 HP, 30s timer.
   - Guard & Guard-Break mechanics: The boss will raise his crossed arms to defend. Light slaps are blocked, but a **Tight Slap**, **Punch**, or **Kick** will shatter his guard and stun him!
   - Victory star ratings (S-Rank, A-Rank, B-Rank) based on time left and maximum streak.

2. **⏱️ 30s Challenge Rush**:
   - High-speed 30-second time trial! Rack up the highest score possible before the timer runs out!

3. **🎨 Free Play Practice**:
   - Infinite sandbox with unlimited HP and no time limit. Freely cycle through all 4 arenas and test combo chains.

### 🎭 Dynamic Boss Moods & Expressions (মুড ও প্রতিক্রিয়া)
- **😎 Smug**: Arrogant half-closed eyes and smirking grin while HP > 65%.
- **😰 Nervous**: Shifty eyes, furrowed brow, and sweat beads rolling down temples as HP drops between 30% - 65%.
- **😭 Begging / Terrified**: Wide trembling pupils, comic tears pouring from eyes, and trembling open mouth when HP < 30%!
- **🛡️ Guarding**: Crossed arms with glowing cyan energy shield and clenched teeth grimace.
- **💫 Stunned**: Dizzy spiral eyes, orbiting cartoon stars, and stunned stumbling recoil upon guard break!

### 🎮 The Characters
1. **The Muscle Bodybuilder (Player)**:
   - Muscular physique with bulging biceps, traps, and chest
   - Fitted athletic shirt showing pecs and core
   - Dark athletic pants and crimson sneakers
   - Dynamic attack animations: open-palm **Slap**, heavyweight **Tight Slap**, straight **Punch**, and roundhouse **Kick**!

2. **The Beard Guy (Target)**:
   - Traditional elegant **White Punjabi (Kurta)** with Mandarin collar and gold buttons
   - Prominent **long flowing beard** that swings and flutters dynamically with inertia
   - Responsive cartoon physics: cheek slap marks, wobbles, dizzy spiral eyes, stumbling steps, and orbiting cartoon stars!

## 🕹️ Controls

| Move | Keyboard Keys | Description | Points |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **SLAP** | `1` or `Q` | Rapid sting slap with quick combo builder | +10 pts |
| **TIGHT SLAP** | `2` or `W` | Massive windup thunder slap with screen rumble & handprint! | +35 pts |
| **PUNCH** | `3` or `E` | Muscular straight gut punch, bending him double | +25 pts |
| **KICK** | `4` or `R` | Powerful roundhouse kick launching him backward | +50 pts |
| **RAPID BARRAGE** | `SPACE` | Unleashes a 10-hit comical flurry finisher (requires full Rage) | +350+ pts |
| **SOUND TOGGLE** | `M` | Mute / Unmute synthesized arcade sound effects | - |
| **RESET** | `ESC` | Resets scores, combo, and characters | - |

### 📱 Mobile & Touch Controls
- **Direct Tap Attack**: Tap directly on the target character on screen to slap him!
- **Swipe Right/Horizontal**: Unleashes a windup **Tight Slap**!
- **Swipe Down**: Delivers a muscle gut **Punch**!
- **Swipe Up**: Launches a roundhouse **Kick**!
- **Zero-Latency Touch Buttons**: 0ms delay mobile-optimized action cards with haptic vibration feedback.
- **Fullscreen Mode**: Tap `⛶ Full` to experience an immersive arcade cabinet view on your phone or tablet!

## 🚀 4 Iconic Bangladesh-Based Arenas (এরিনা সমাহার)

1. 🏠 **BD Baithak (বাংলাদেশি বৈঠকখানা / ড্রয়িং রুম)**:
   - Pistachio mint distemper walls with classic plaster dado border.
   - Large window with authentic wrought-iron geometric grills (লোহার গ্রিল), tied floral curtains, and scenic outdoor view (banana leaves, coconut tree, tin roof with a pigeon).
   - Slow-spinning ceiling fan (সিলিং ফ্যান) with real-time 3D perspective rotation.
   - Classic Bangla Wall Calendar (১৪৩১ বঙ্গাব্দ, গ্রামীণ দৃশ্য) hanging on the wall.
   - Fluorescent tube light with ambient soft glare, bakelite switchboard with glowing red LED indicator, red RFL plastic chair with gamcha, and a burning mosquito coil with rising curling smoke!
   - Glazed red-oxide floor with reflections.

2. ☕ **Tong Er Dokan (চায়ের টং দোকান ও রিকশা আড্ডা)**:
   - Atmospheric Dhaka roadside tea stall under a corrugated tin shed (টিনের চালা) and bamboo poles.
   - Steaming aluminium tea kettle (কেটলি) puffing steam into the air, hanging bananas (সবরি কলা কাঁদি), biscuit jars with red lids, and condensed milk cans!
   - Brightly painted traditional Rickshaw with vibrant Rickshaw Art and folded hood parked in the background.
   - Tangled overhead electric wire jungle (তারের জটলা) and hanging yellow tungsten bulb.

3. 🪁 **Puran Dhaka (পুরান ঢাকার ছাদ ও শাকরাইন ঘুড়ি উৎসব)**:
   - Shakrain festive dusk sunset over dense historic brick architecture, minarets, and mosques.
   - Colorful paper kites (চিল ঘুড়ি, চোখদার ঘুড়ি) flying across the sky with trailing ribbons and spool string lines (সুতা).
   - Rooftop clotheslines with drying checked lungis and sarees fluttering in the wind.
   - Black Gazi Water Tank (গাজী পানির ট্যাংক), wooden naatai (নাটাই), and weathered brick parapets.

4. 🚢 **Sadarghat Launch Ghat (সদরঘাট লঞ্চ টার্মিনাল ও বুড়িগঙ্গা)**:
   - Flowing Buriganga river with floating water hyacinths (কচুরিপানা) and wooden country boats (নৌকা ও মাঝি).
   - Giant triple-decker passenger launch (*এম.ভি. সোনার বাংলা*) docked alongside with illuminated cabin windows, captain bridge, smoke funnel, and fluttering Bangladesh red-green national flag!
   - Floating metal pontoon pier with mooring ropes and iron bollards.

- 👥 **Interactive Crowd & Dynamic Banter System**:
  - **Left Mob Faction**: Bystanders shouting `"AROH DEN!"`, `"MAAR SHALA RE!"`, `"AAR EKTA DE!"`, `"CHHARBI NA!"`, `"LIVE CHHALAISI!"` while recording video on a glowing smartphone with a blinking `● LIVE` indicator!

  - Click on the crowd on either side to trigger responsive crowd banter!

- 🔥 **High-Stakes Streak Engine**:
  - Land consecutive hits to build your streak:
    - **5x**: ON FIRE (1.5x Multiplier) + Fiery golden aura!
    - **10x**: ULTRA RAGE STREAK (2.0x Multiplier)
    - **20x**: THAPRANO MONSTER (3.0x Multiplier)
    - **35x**: GODLIKE SLAPPER (5.0x Multiplier)
  - Streak Timer Bar: You have ~3.2 seconds between strikes to keep the streak alive. If it runs out, your streak breaks with a comic wah-wah effect!

- 🥋 **Enhanced Character Visuals & Physics**:
  - **Bodybuilder**: Striated muscles, athletic fade haircut, fierce expression, vascular bicep cuts, gym lion emblem, sweat wristbands, and streak flame auras!
  - **Beard Panjabi Guy**: White silk kurta with golden buttons and embroidered Mandarin collar, dynamic flowing beard physics, flying prayer cap (টুপি) that launches into the air on heavy slaps, flying slipper on kicks, sweat beads, and 5-finger red handprint slap marks!
